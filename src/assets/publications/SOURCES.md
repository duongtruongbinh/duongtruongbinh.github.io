# Publication figures

Figures are complete WebP copies of the authors' original figures, taken from the highest-resolution source available and trimmed of surrounding whitespace. Do not crop them to a sub-region.

- `counterfactual-reasoning-robust-vqa.webp`: Figure 1, page 3 of [the paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.16567), rendered from the vector PDF at 400 dpi.
- `horse-rm.webp`: Figure 1, page 2 of the author-supplied `m58126-trung paper.pdf`. The PDF was removed after extraction at the author's request.
- `lvt-eg.webp`: [Pipeline figure from the official repository](https://github.com/Kein95/lvt-eg/blob/main/assets/pipeline.png).
- `viinfographicvqa.webp`: Figure 2, [original from the official repository](https://github.com/duongtruongbinh/ViInfographicVQA/blob/main/assets/samples.png).
- `vivqa-x.webp`: [Pipeline figure from the official repository](https://github.com/duongtruongbinh/ViVQA-X/blob/main/assets/ViVQA-X-pipeline.png).
- `dam-qa.webp`: Figure 2, page 3 of [the paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.12441), rendered from the vector PDF at 400 dpi (the repository copy is only 849 px wide).
