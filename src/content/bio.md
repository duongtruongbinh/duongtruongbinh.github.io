I am an M.Sc. student in Telecommunications at the **Institut national de la recherche scientifique (INRS)**, supervised by [Prof. Long Bao Le](https://longbaole.github.io/). My research focuses on **world models, vision-language-action (VLA) models, and embodied AI for robot learning**, with broader interests in multimodal and robust/generalizable AI.

Previously, I worked on robust visual question answering and multimodal learning. I received my B.Sc. in Information Technology from **VNUHCM - University of Science** in 2025 and also contribute to research and teaching at **AI VIETNAM**.
