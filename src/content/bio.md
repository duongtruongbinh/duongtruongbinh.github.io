I am an M.Sc. student in Telecommunications at the [Institut national de la recherche scientifique (INRS)](https://inrs.ca/), supervised by [Prof. Long Bao Le](https://longbaole.github.io/). My research focuses on **world models and vision-language-action (VLA) models for robot learning**: how embodied AI systems can learn to act and generalize across environments.

My earlier work centered on robust visual question answering and multimodal learning.
