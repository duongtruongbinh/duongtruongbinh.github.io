I am an M.Sc. student in Telecommunications at the [Institut national de la recherche scientifique (INRS)](https://inrs.ca/), supervised by [Prof. Long Bao Le](https://longbaole.github.io/). My research focuses on **world models and vision-language-action (VLA) models for robot learning**, building on my previous work in robust visual question answering and multimodal learning.

I am interested in how embodied AI systems can learn to act and generalize across environments. I received my B.Sc. in Information Technology from VNUHCM - University of Science in 2025 and contribute to research and teaching at [AI VIETNAM](https://aivietnam.edu.vn/).
